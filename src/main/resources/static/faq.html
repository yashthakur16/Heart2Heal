Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Organ donation is the process of giving an organ or tissue to a person in need of a transplant. It can be done by a living donor or after death.
Anyone, regardless of age or medical history, can register as an organ donor. However, the suitability of organs is determined at the time of donation.
The most commonly donated organs include the heart, liver, kidneys, lungs, pancreas, and intestines. Tissues such as corneas, skin, bones, and heart valves can also be donated.

Most major religions encourage or permit organ donation as an act of charity and saving lives. It’s advisable to check with your religious leader if you have concerns.
