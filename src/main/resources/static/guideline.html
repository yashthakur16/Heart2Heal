Organ Donation Guidelines

Eligibility Criteria for Organ Donation

  • Living donors must be in good physical and mental health, usually between the ages of 18 and 60.
  • Deceased donors can donate after brain death or cardiac death, subject to medical evaluation.
  • Some medical conditions may prevent donation, such as cancer or severe infections.

Types of Organ Donation

  • Living Donation: A healthy individual donates a kidney, part of the liver, or a lung lobe.
  • Deceased Donation: Organs and tissues are retrieved from a donor after death with consent.

Legal & Ethical Considerations

  • Consent from the donor or next of kin is required.
  • Selling or buying organs is illegal in most countries.
  • Organ allocation is managed through a fair and transparent waiting list system.
