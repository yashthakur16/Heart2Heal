Tips for Successful Organ Donation

For Living Donors

  • Maintain good health with regular check-ups before donation.
  • Consult a doctor to understand the risks and benefits.
  • Ensure psychological readiness for donation.
  • Follow post-donation care instructions strictly.

For Deceased Donors & Their Families

  • Discuss your organ donation decision with your family.
  • Register as an organ donor to make the process easier.
  • Stay informed about your country’s organ donation laws and policies.

For Organ Recipients

  • Follow your doctor’s instructions carefully post-transplant.
  • Take prescribed medications to prevent organ rejection.
  • Adopt a healthy lifestyle to ensure long-term organ function.
